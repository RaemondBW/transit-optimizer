    Market St trunk

    Under Market St, five Metro lines (J, K, L, M, N) share one subway. Every train has to merge in at a portal, and a delay on any branch spills into the tunnel for everyone. What if one line ran in the subway, as a frequent, evenly spaced shuttle between Embarcadero and West Portal, and the other lines ended at the subway, where riders transfer underground? The same idea works for buses that drive the length of Market St above it.

    Metro: one line in the subway

    Buses: end at a subway station

    Several bus routes also run the length of Market St, directly above the subway. If they ended at a station and riders transferred down to the trains, the buses saved could run more often on the rest of each route.
